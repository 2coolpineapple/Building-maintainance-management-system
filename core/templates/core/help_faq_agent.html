Help & FAQ

You can check train timings on the official Indian Railways website or mobile app.

Tickets can be booked online via IRCTC or at railway stations.

Refunds are processed as per the Indian Railways refund policy available on their website.

Luggage allowance varies by class; please refer to the official guidelines.

For emergencies, dial 100. For customer support, call +91 12345 67890.

You can track your complaint status by entering your complaint ID on the 'Track Complaint' page.

You can submit a new complaint by navigating to the 'Submit Complaint' page and filling out the form.

For urgent maintenance issues, please contact the assigned officer or the maintenance department directly.

You can provide feedback by clicking the 'Provide Feedback' button on the complaint detail page after your complaint is resolved.

Resolution times vary depending on the nature of the complaint. You can check the status history on the complaint detail page for updates.
